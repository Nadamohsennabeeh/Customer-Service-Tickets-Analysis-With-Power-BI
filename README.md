# Customer Service Performance & Ticket Analysis | Power BI

Find the live version here: https://app.powerbi.com/groups/me/reports/9fd7fff8-7569-4fc2-91f9-4deb51f4808c/f1aab6ca011b1db6e431?experience=power-bi

This Power BI dashboard analyzes customer service tickets from August to September. It focuses on ticket resolution, response time, customer satisfaction, and common customer issues.

## Key Dimensions & Features

**Performance KPIs:** Total Tickets, Average Response Time, Average Resolution Time, and Customer Satisfaction Score.

**Ticket Outcomes:** Analyzes Resolved, Not Resolved, and Escalated tickets.

**First-Contact Resolution:** Measures tickets solved during the first customer contact.

**Issue Analysis:** Identifies common issues and their root causes.

**Unresolved Tickets:** Analyzes open tickets by priority and channel.

**Escalated Tickets:** Shows escalated tickets by issue, priority, and channel.

**Repeat Contact:** Tracks customers who contact support again within 7 days.

**Monthly Trends:** Compares ticket volume across August and September.

**Interactive Filters:** Filters by Priority, Channel, and Month.

**Data Visualization:** Used KPI cards, bar charts, donut charts, and a decomposition tree.

**Dashboard Design:** Created three pages: Overview, Not Solved, and Escalated.
